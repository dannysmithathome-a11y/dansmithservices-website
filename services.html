Services Offered

Fractional leadership and hands-on delivery.

Helping growing SaaS businesses simplify, standardise and scale their Go-to-Market operations. Seven areas of experience, each available on a consultancy or fractional basis — process, forecasting, technology, commercial operations, compensation, data and AI-enabled automation.

01

GTM Process & Operating Model Design

End-to-end GTM process design

Mapping and redesigning the revenue process across Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Finance and Product, defining roles, handoffs and SLAs.

Process documentation & SOPs

Turning undocumented working practice into written standards teams can actually follow.

GTM policy & standards definition

Setting the operating rules for how deals, data and decisions move through the business.

Change management & adoption

Structured rollout, training and reinforcement so new process sticks rather than lapsing.

02

Forecasting & Pipeline Management

Forecast accuracy improvement

Diagnosing why the number moves and rebuilding the methodology behind it.

Sales stage criteria & pipeline hygiene

Defining exit criteria, cleaning the pipeline and putting the routines in place to keep it clean.

Forecast cadence & governance

Designing the weekly and monthly review rhythm, roll-up structure and accountability.

Channel & partner forecasting

Extending the same discipline to indirect sellers and dealer networks.

03

CRM & Revenue Technology

Revenue tech stack assessment & rationalisation

Auditing what you own, what it costs and what can be consolidated.

CRM optimisation & governance

Configuration, data model, permissions and the admin discipline to keep it healthy.

Platform migration & consolidation

Planning and running tooling moves without stalling the sales team.

Requirements translation

Converting commercial requirements into a specification technology teams and vendors can build against.

04

Deal Desk & Commercial Excellence

Deal desk design & operation

Standing up the function, its approval matrix and its service levels.

Quote, proposal & approval workflow design

Including document automation integrated with the CRM, as delivered with Xpertdoc at Visiopharm.

Pricing & commercial policy

Designing pricing rules, discount governance and the reporting to prove the impact.

Lead-to-cash optimisation

Removing friction across the full commercial cycle from enquiry to invoice.

05

Sales Compensation, Territory & Channel Operations

Sales compensation plan design

Building plans that align to commercial goals and can be administered without argument.

Commission plan governance

Documentation, approval routes and dispute handling.

Territory design & coverage planning

Allocating accounts and coverage across direct and indirect teams.

Channel & partner operations

Partner portals, opportunity registration and indirect seller process.

06

Data, Metrics & Reporting

Revenue metric definition & single source of truth

Agreeing what each number means and where it comes from.

Executive & board reporting

Translating operational models into the language leadership makes decisions in.

Pipeline & performance dashboards

Building the reporting layer in Salesforce, Clari or Power BI.

Data governance & CRM hygiene

The rules and routines that stop reporting quality decaying.

07

AI & Automation for Revenue Operations

AI opportunity assessment

Identifying which revenue operations tasks are genuinely worth automating, and which are not.

Reporting & admin automation

Replacing recurring manual reporting with automated production.

AI-assisted triage & routing

Categorisation and assignment logic for operational request queues.

AI adoption & enablement

Getting a commercial team to actually use the tools, with the guardrails to do it safely.
